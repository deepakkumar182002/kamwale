Common Questions

How long does a project take?

It depends on scope — a simple website takes 1–2 weeks, while complex apps can take 2–4 months. I will provide a clear timeline in the proposal.

Do you offer revisions?

Yes — all projects include revision rounds at each milestone. I work until you're satisfied with the result.

What payment methods do you accept?

I accept bank transfer, UPI, and international payments via Wise or PayPal. I work on a milestone-based payment plan.

Do you provide post-launch support?

Yes — I offer a free 30-day support period after launch, and ongoing maintenance packages for clients who need continued assistance.

Can I hire you for just design (no development)?

Absolutely. I provide standalone UI/UX design, logo design, and graphics services independently of development work.

How do we communicate during a project?

You can reach me via WhatsApp, email, or a project management tool like Notion or Trello — whatever works best for you. Regular updates are guaranteed.

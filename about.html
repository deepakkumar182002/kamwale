About Me

Deepak Kumar
Software Engineer

I am an independent software engineer with 3+ years of experience, helping businesses build clean, reliable, and practical digital products.

My Journey

Built on the belief that great software creates real business value

I started my work with a simple goal: make high-quality digital services accessible to businesses that need practical and dependable technology. I began with web development and expanded into mobile apps, UI/UX, graphics, and 3D modelling.

Today, I work with startups, SMEs, and established brands across multiple industries, delivering solutions that are not just visually polished but technically robust and easy to scale.

Every project, regardless of size, gets focused attention, transparent communication, and a commitment to quality delivery.

Kamwale.tech is my future growth vision, and I am expanding it step by step from now. As the owner, I plan to formally launch Kamwale.tech in the future as a complete software solutions brand.

Currently, I work on CMS, admin panels, websites, mobile apps, UI/UX, and 3D model design. In the future, I will expand into CRM platforms, advanced CMS solutions, AI-based industry projects, management systems, dashboards, and AI agentic project development.
